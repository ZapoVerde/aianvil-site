About

A workshop, not a platform.

AI Anvil is a personal workshop for AI projects built at home. It is deliberately small: one household, a handful of tools, and no ambition to become a public service.

BigBrain

The first project under the AI Anvil name is BigBrain, a self-hosted household assistant. BigBrain brings together a shared calendar, lists and meal plans, recipes, notes, documents, and conversational help in one place. It can keep a two-way sync with Google Calendar, so events added in either place stay in step.

BigBrain runs on hardware operated by the household. Its data lives in a self-hosted database, and the AI model it uses is chosen by the operator. It is available only to invited household members through a private access gate.

How it is run

  • Invitation only. There is no public registration or login page advertised here.
  • No advertising. Nothing on this site or in the app is monetised through ads.
  • No data business. Personal data is not sold or used to train advertising profiles.
  • Repairs over releases. Tools are built to be useful at home, then maintained.
