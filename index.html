AI Anvil is the home of BigBrain, a self-hosted assistant built for one household. No public sign-up. No advertising. No data business.

Invitation-only, by design.

Principles

Private by default

Your calendar, notes, and questions are not a product. Access is limited to the people who live here.

Self-hosted

BigBrain runs on hardware we control, stores its data in our own database, and connects only to services we choose.

Small on purpose

Built for a handful of people, not a market. Features earn their place by being useful at home.

The first project

BigBrain

BigBrain is a household assistant for the everyday stuff: a shared calendar, shopping and to-do lists, recipes, notes, documents, and plain-language questions. It keeps a two-way sync with Google Calendar and can use an AI model you configure to help make sense of it all.

It is not a public service. There is no app store listing, no sign-up form, and no growth loop — just a small tool that makes a home run a little smoother.

What it handles

  • Household calendar and Google Calendar sync
  • Lists, shopping, and meal plans
  • Recipes and notes
  • Document search and chat
